## Typography and semantic safeguards

- Preserve C* and * notation with the existing aljabar-$C^*$,
  subaljabar-$C^*$, homomorfisme-$*$, and isomorfisme-$*$ backend forms.
- Translate “isometric” as isometrik, “contractive” as kontraktif, and
  “dense” as padat unless a sentence requires a more explicit phrase.
- Use pemetaan for map/function language and operator when the source
  explicitly says operator; do not collapse a character into an ordinary
  evaluation function.
- Preserve theorem names as proper names (Teorema Wiener, Teorema
  Stone--Weierstrass, Teorema Gelfand--Naimark) and keep source labels and
  citation keys unchanged.
- The single Xy-pic diagram needs an accessible prose description; its labels
  are mathematical identifiers, not translatable prose.
