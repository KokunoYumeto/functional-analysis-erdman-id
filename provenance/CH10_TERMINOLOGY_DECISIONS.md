# Chapter 10 terminology decision — tempered distribution

Date: 2026-08-22  
Unit: `FAOA-2015-CH10`  
Decision: **preferred Indonesian term = `distribusi tempered`**

The one-time cross-chapter terminology gate used the official UNDIP JFMA
fallback and remains valid. During contiguous Chapter 10 production, one
additional source was inspected solely to resolve the chapter-specific term
*tempered distribution*:

- official host: ITB Digital Library;
- official PDF:
  `https://digilib.itb.ac.id/assets/files/2019/2018_TS_PP_PEBRUDAL_ZANU_1-BAB2.pdf`;
- internal title: *Bab II: Ruang dan Operator LPS*;
- local evidence path:
  `qa/terminology_evidence/itb-distribusi-tempered-2018-bab2.pdf`;
- identity: 283,518 bytes / 10 pages / SHA-256
  `830a241c8ace73290a4c613cc6478bb17698d835b781b1fec332fa09838ddf02`;
- inspected surface: PDF page 2, section II.1.3.

The page visibly uses `Distribusi Tempered` as the section heading and
`distribusi tempered` in the defining prose for continuous linear
functionals on the Schwartz space. This is direct Indonesian field-usage
evidence, so it outweighs the provisional calque `distribusi temper`.

The Chapter 10 translation therefore uses `distribusi tempered` as the
preferred reader term. Where Erdman explicitly gives *temperate
distributions* as an alternative English name, the translation preserves that
source distinction as the recognition form `distribusi temperate`. No
admitted Chapter 1--9 prose contains either term, so no retroactive reader
change is required.

The ITB PDF is retained as local QA evidence only. Its redistribution rights
have not been established, so it is excluded from public release payloads.
This decision changes terminology, not mathematical content or source credit.
