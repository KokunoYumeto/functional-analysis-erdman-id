# Source authority and selected corpus

Frozen: 2026-08-21

This edition derives from John M. Erdman's *Functional Analysis and Operator
Algebras: An Introduction*, version 4 October 2015, obtained from the author's
official Portland State University page:

- `https://web.pdx.edu/~erdman/`
- `https://web.pdx.edu/~erdman/FAOA/functional_analysis_operator_algebras_web.zip`
- `https://web.pdx.edu/~erdman/FAOA/functional_analysis_operator_algebras_pdf.pdf`

There is no official source repository, tag, commit, or immutable release.
Authority is therefore bound to the official URLs, internal version, byte

The selected corpus is the complete published book: 17 chapters in source
order, preface, bibliography, and index. Chapters 1–8 are the D20 core route;
Chapters 9–17 are retained as the advanced continuation. Commented prospective
K1/groupoid material is not part of the selected source.

The exact Chapter 1 authority is `linalg.tex`, 58,673 bytes / 1,261 lines /
SHA-256 `a15cabf306adf5457cedce046f98b9474c72b38ab50197b0dc4288e942772096`.
